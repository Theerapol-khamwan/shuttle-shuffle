#### Overall Impression
The app excels in aesthetic execution and domain-specific logic, creating a highly functional offline-first tool. The single biggest opportunity is refining the cognitive load during complex input moments—specifically post-game cost inputs and pre-game setup.

#### What's Working
- **Uncompromising Design Execution:** The Neo-Brutalist aesthetic is flawlessly executed. `NeoButton` physical translation (`translateX: 4, translateY: 4`) matches the solid shadow perfectly.
- **Context-Aware Ergonomics:** The scoreboard gracefully adapts to landscape mode, ensuring "Clarity from a distance," ideal for a physical scoreboard at the back of the court.
- **Domain-Specific Problem Solving:** Features like `bulkMatchGeneration` and Equal vs. Pro-rata cost splitting directly eliminate the most annoying frictions of organizing a badminton group.

#### Priority Issues
- **[P1] Hidden Court Configuration**: `total_courts` defaults to 1 and is buried in a modal.
  - **Why it matters**: It dictates the entire queue flow, and users might miss it until they've already started.
  - **Fix**: Expose court configuration prominently during session setup or the empty dashboard state.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable layout`

- **[P1] Sweaty Hands vs. Tiny Inputs**: The cost input row relies on standard digital keyboards and tiny `Switch` toggles.
  - **Why it matters**: Sweaty fingers lead to typos, and tiny toggles violate the "Large touch targets" rule.
  - **Fix**: Use chunky, neo-brutalist `+` and `-` steppers for things like hours and shuttle counts.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable shape`

- **[P2] Scoreboard Menu Hitbox**: The scoreboard's floating hamburger menu uses a standard icon size.
  - **Why it matters**: Difficult to hit accurately during a fast-paced game.
  - **Fix**: Increase the hit area and visually anchor it with a larger button target.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable polish`

- **[P2] Waitlist Sorting Logic**: The waitlist (`waitingPlayers`) sorts only by `games_played`.
  - **Why it matters**: Players with the same number of games appear in arbitrary order, perceived as unfair.
  - **Fix**: Factor in "longest waiting time" for sorting.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable optimize`

#### Persona Red Flags
**Casey (Distracted Mobile User)**: Using phone one-handed on the go, sweaty hands.
- The tiny toggle switch for "Equal vs. Pro-rata" is hard to hit accurately.
- Keyboard input for numeric values (hours, shuttles) causes frustration and potential errors compared to large stepper buttons.

**Jordan (First-Timer)**: Needs guidance at every step.
- Court count setting is hidden inside a gear icon; Jordan might start a session without realizing they can add multiple courts.
- Lack of onboarding means Jordan might not know how to edit a score if they make a mistake.

#### Minor Observations
- The fallback logic for premium Thai text rendering (`isThaiText` regex) is a brilliant typographic detail.
- Semantic token names like "Grip Tape Black" and "High-Visibility Volt" are excellent.

#### Questions to Consider
- If the app acts as the scoreboard for every match, couldn't the app calculate `hoursPlayed` automatically based on the timestamps of the first and last match?
- Instead of counting shuttles manually, what if the app tracked elapsed time and suggested an estimated shuttle count?
