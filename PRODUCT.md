# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Badminton groups and friends who play together and need an easy way to manage queueing, matchmaking, and scoring. They use this app directly by the court, often leaving the phone in landscape mode to act as a physical scoreboard.

## Product Purpose
To serve as an offline-first, fair, and smart queue manager and scoreboard. It prevents repetitive matchmaking, ensures fair playtime, calculates costs accurately, and removes the confusion of keeping score, acting as both an organizer and a referee in a single device.

## Brand Personality
Playful Neo-Brutalist. Energetic, bold, high-contrast, clear, and unpretentious. It uses thick borders, solid shadows, and a bright "Electric Yellow" to feel alive and sporty.

## Anti-references
- Corporate, overly formal, or bland SaaS designs.
- Over-designed complex UI that is hard to read from a distance.
- Fragile glassmorphism that reduces legibility.

## Design Principles
- **Clarity from a distance:** Elements must be readable at a glance from the back of the court.
- **Unapologetically bold:** Use stark contrast and neo-brutalist aesthetics to make interactions obvious.
- **Function over decoration:** Every UI element serves the game; avoid decorative elements that don't help manage the match or score.
- **Resilient and offline:** The app should feel instantaneous and reliable, saving state perfectly without a network.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- **High-Visibility & Contrast:** Essential for viewing on a phone screen from across a badminton court.
- **Large touch targets:** For sweaty hands interacting with the score quickly during a game.
